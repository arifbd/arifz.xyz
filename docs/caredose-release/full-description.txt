Was that dose already given? What's due next?

CareDose helps you keep track of medicines for yourself and the people you care for. Put each person's schedule in one place, get reminders on your phone and keep a record of doses given or skipped.

A DAILY PLAN FOR YOUR FAMILY
Create separate profiles for yourself, your children, parents or other family members. Add medicines, dose amounts, times and course dates from the prescription. Keep food instructions and notes alongside each medicine.

REMINDERS YOU CAN ACT ON
Set up medicine reminders, then mark a dose as given or skipped from the notification, or snooze it for ten minutes. CareDose can send a follow-up when a scheduled dose is still unrecorded.

Reminder delivery depends on your phone's notification permissions, alarm access and battery settings. The app includes guidance to help you check them.

A RECORD TO LOOK BACK AT
Check dose history when you need to know what was recorded and when. Add who gave the dose, leave a note or correct a record. A weekly summary brings together given, skipped and missed doses based on the schedules and records in the app.

PRESCRIPTIONS CLOSE AT HAND
Attach a prescription photo from your phone's photo picker to a medicine. Keep tablets, syrups, drops and other medicine forms organised with the details you enter.

SHARE A COPY OF THE PLAN
Send a medicine schedule or weekly summary through an app you choose. It's useful when someone else needs the details. Shared copies do not update automatically; this release does not sync records between caregivers' phones.

OFFLINE, WITH NO SIGN-UP
Profiles, medicine schedules, dose tracking and local reminders work offline. There are no ads or analytics SDKs. Your care records are stored in the app's private storage. Google Play updates and external sharing or storage services may use the internet.

BACK UP BEFORE YOU CHANGE PHONES
Export a backup file containing your records and prescription photos, then restore it when needed. You can also choose a folder for optional weekly backups. CareDose does not encrypt these files, so keep them private. Android automatic backup is disabled for app data.

USE A LANGUAGE YOU KNOW
Choose from 19 languages, including English, Bangla, Hindi, Urdu, Arabic, Spanish, French and more. Change the language inside the app at any time.

Want to look around first? Choose “Explore with sample data” on the welcome screen. No account or real health information is needed.

IMPORTANT HEALTH INFORMATION
CareDose is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. It does not calculate doses or replace professional medical advice. Follow your prescription and consult a qualified healthcare professional about medicines, missed doses or treatment decisions. Do not rely on the app as your only reminder for time-critical medication.
