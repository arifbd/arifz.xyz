BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few things
to know.

Are the stories in English or Bangla?

The stories are in Bangla. This page describes the Bengali reading experience in English.

Is every story free?

Some stories are free. Others offer the first two chapters free, with later chapters unlocked using diamonds or a full-story purchase. Review the offer and price in the app before buying.

Can I read without internet?

Downloaded story content can be read offline. Content downloads, purchases and other online features need a connection.

Can I return to my story later?

Yes. Save your progress and continue later. Progress is stored on your device; clearing storage or changing devices can affect saves. Read the privacy policy for backup details.

Where can I read about privacy and purchases?

See the Privacy Policy for data handling and the Terms of Service for purchases, refunds and support.
