Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

1. Overview

GolpoTwist is a Bengali interactive storytelling app developed by Md Nazmul Haque Arif, operating under the name arifz (“we”, “us”). This policy explains local storage, backups, purchases, diagnostics and your choices. The Android package is xyz.arifz.golpotwist. You do not need a developer-operated account. Available story text and saved progress can be used offline; purchases, media downloads, system backups and enabled diagnostics use online services. Advertising is disabled in the current release.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores story progress and choices, save slots, reading and scroll positions, unlocked endings, chapter and hint unlocks, extra save slots, diamond balances and earning/spending records, daily reward claims, current and best streaks, and purchase-related records in private app storage. Records can include story or product identifiers, timestamps and, for older diamond purchases, purchase tokens used to prevent duplicate credits. These records support continuing stories, rewards and purchased access.

Reading preferences include text size, line spacing, theme, screen brightness, orientation and keeping the reader screen on. We also store audio levels, reminder and privacy choices, and which media packs you have removed. The reward system uses the device's date, time zone, boot count and elapsed time locally to limit repeated reward claims. These clock records are not a GPS location or a persistent device identifier.

Story artwork, music and sound effects are app content, not recordings or files collected from your personal media library. We do not operate a cloud account or our own server for synchronizing saved stories. Android backup can transfer progress and settings off the device as explained in section 8; selected usage events can also be sent to Firebase as explained below.

3. Advertising and consent

The current release does not request or display banner, chapter-end or rewarded ads, and does not offer Remove Ads for sale. Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform libraries remain included, but the app's advertising and advertising-consent flows are disabled. This does not disable the separate Firebase, Google Play or Android backup features described in this policy.

If you use an earlier version with advertising enabled, Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform handle advertising and consent. In those versions, the advertising SDK may process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device and advertising identifiers (including advertising ID and app set ID), app interactions, ad views and clicks, and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Personalized advertising depends on your consent, region and Google settings; non-personalized ads can still process data. Where offered, use Settings → advertising and privacy options to review or change advertising consent. Android also provides advertising privacy controls. Advertising consent is separate from the analytics switch. App set ID is used for essential purposes such as analytics and fraud prevention, not ad personalization or ad measurement; we do not combine it with personal information or other device identifiers for advertising purposes.

In versions with advertising enabled, the Remove Ads purchase removes the placements described in the store. Optional rewarded ads remain available, and the advertising SDK may prepare them in advance. Buying Remove Ads does not by itself switch off analytics or all advertising-SDK processing. If advertising is introduced in a later release, we will update the relevant disclosures and provide any consent choices required before that processing begins.

4. Analytics and crash reports

When enabled, Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics process app/device information, app-instance or installation identifiers, usage events and crash diagnostics to understand usage and improve reliability. This can include app version, device model, operating system, interaction times, crash traces and diagnostic logs. Online requests expose an IP address to the receiving service; Analytics can derive approximate geographic information from network information without accessing GPS.

Custom events include story and chapter identifiers, save-slot number and whether a story was resumed, starts and completions, whether a choice used diamonds, endings reached or shared, where a reading session stopped, paywalls shown, media-download identifiers and error codes, and product identifiers and purchase outcomes. SDKs can also generate automatic usage and diagnostic events. These records are not necessarily anonymous merely because they do not include your name. The app does not deliberately send story text or a personal account profile in its custom events. Analytics advertising-ID collection and analytics advertising-personalization signals are disabled in the app configuration.

5. Your analytics choice

On a fresh installation, analytics storage consent and automatic crash-report uploads default to denied/off. The app uses SIM country, network country or device locale to decide whether to ask for analytics consent in the EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland; it also asks if the country cannot be determined. Elsewhere, the current app may enable analytics and crash reporting unless you decline. This country check does not use GPS and may not reflect your physical location.

Change the usage-information switch in Settings (গোপনীয়তা → বেনামী ব্যবহারের তথ্য পাঠাও) at any time. Turning it off sets analytics storage consent to denied and disables automatic crash-report uploads. This is not a guarantee of zero network traffic: Google's consent mode can send limited consent and measurement signals without stored analytics identifiers. Previously transmitted data is not automatically deleted. Crash reports can remain locally queued and may be uploaded if reporting is later enabled. Saved consent choices can persist between launches.

Declining analytics does not prevent reading stories or receiving daily rewards. It does not disable purchases, media downloads, feedback you send or Android backup. In versions with ads, advertising consent is controlled separately. See Google's explanation of consent mode.

6. Purchases and Google Play

Google Play Billing handles payment for digital products. Google processes payment/account information under its own policies; we do not receive your payment-card details through the app. The app processes product identifiers, purchase tokens, purchase status, signed purchase information and entitlements to validate and deliver purchases, restore access and avoid duplicate credits. Purchase checks run when the app opens and when you request a restore. Current purchases unlock individual stories or fixed story volumes; older purchases can include Remove Ads or the retired diamond pack. We do not sell diamonds in the current release. Google Play may retain transaction records independently of app deletion.

Google Play Asset Delivery handles story artwork, music and sound downloads and may process device, app and delivery information. The app can automatically request missing media when it opens or a story opens, using network data and storage subject to Google Play's delivery controls. Removing a downloadable media pack frees its media storage without deleting the story's text or progress. The app remembers that removal and excludes the pack from automatic prefetch until you request it again.

7. Permissions, reminders and sharing

Internet and network-state access support online features. Optional notification permission enables reminders about unfinished stories; you can disable reminders in the app or Android settings. Reminders are scheduled locally from saved progress and can show a story title and chapter on your notification or lock screen, depending on your Android settings.

When you choose to share an ending, the app creates an image in its private cache and opens Android's share sheet. The image and accompanying text can reveal the story, ending and how many endings you have found. Only the destination you choose receives the selected content through that action; the destination handles it under its own policy and may publish it to the audience you select. The cached image can remain until replaced, cleared by Android or removed with app data. The app does not require access to contacts, microphone, camera or precise device location for storytelling. Included service libraries also use advertising/attribution permissions and background-work permissions for advertising measurement, uploads and content delivery; these do not grant access to your contacts or precise location.

8. Backup, retention and deletion

The app asks Android's backup service to save a progress snapshot when relevant progress changes. The snapshot includes the diamond ledger (including any historical purchase-token records), chapter and hint unlocks, extra slots, reward claims and streaks, saves and choices, endings, reading positions and settings, including privacy choices. Android and your configured backup provider, such as Google, handle the backup; this is separate from a developer-operated cloud account. Older database-and-preference backups can also be restored. Current Google Play purchase entitlements are restored from Play separately.

Backup and restoration depend on device settings, account, available quota, Android scheduling and compatible backup data. Backups may be delayed, missing or older than your latest progress. A restore can replace local progress and settings rather than merge progress from different devices. Restored privacy choices can take effect again; review them in Settings after restoring. We cannot access or delete your system backup for you. Manage or delete existing backups through Android or your backup provider; uninstalling alone does not necessarily delete them.

Local records remain until removed or app storage is cleared. To remove local data, use Android Settings → Apps → GolpoTwist → Storage → Clear storage/data, or uninstall. This can permanently remove progress and earned or historically purchased diamonds and locally earned unlocks. Clearing app storage does not erase Google Play transactions, previously shared content or previously transmitted SDK records.

Google states that Crashlytics retains crash traces and associated installation identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal from live and backup systems. Analytics user/event retention follows the project's configured retention controls for usage and reliability analysis; expiry can reset with new activity where that setting is enabled, and aggregated reporting can follow different retention rules. Contact us to ask about the current retention setting or request deletion of identifiable records we control. Historical advertising and Google Play transaction records follow Google's applicable retention policies. There is no single retention period covering every service. See Firebase retention information and Analytics retention controls. Contact us for deletion requests concerning information we hold; do not send purchase tokens or payment details.

Android app isolation and service-provider transport protections help protect information, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure.
