Privacy Policy for Secure Password Generator

Effective Date: August 16, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how arifz ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in the Secure Password Generator mobile application (the "App"). Your privacy is critically important to us, and this policy is designed to be as simple and transparent as possible.

1. The Core Principle: We Collect No Data

Secure Password Generator is designed with a "privacy-first" approach. The core principle of our App is that we do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or any data generated by the user.

All operations, including password generation and password analysis, are performed exclusively on your device. Nothing ever leaves your phone.

2. Information You Provide to Us

We do not ask you for any personal information. There are no user accounts, no registration forms, and no way for us to identify you.

  • Generated Passwords: Passwords you create using the generator are shown to you on your screen and can be copied to your clipboard. They are never saved by the App or sent to any server.
  • Analyzed Passwords: Passwords you enter into the analyzer feature are processed in real-time on your device to provide strength feedback. This information is processed ephemerally and is never stored or transmitted.

3. Permissions

Secure Password Generator requires no special permissions to function. It does not need access to your contacts, storage, camera, location, or any other sensitive part of your device.

4. Third-Party Services

Our App does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics tools, or advertising networks. There are no third parties involved that could collect data from you.

5. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since the App collects no information whatsoever, we believe it is safe for all audiences.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
